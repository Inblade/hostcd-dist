Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 November 2026.

This policy explains what personal data we process when you visit hostcd.dev or contact us, why, and what rights you have. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to answer you and run the site: no cookies, no advertising, and no cross-site trackers. The only analytics we use is privacy-first and cookieless — it counts page visits and link clicks in aggregate and never identifies you.

Who we are (the controller)

hostcd is an independent software project operated by Danylo Kochetov, based in Poland ("we", "us", "the operator"). For any privacy question, or to exercise the rights below, contact hello@hostcd.dev. We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer, as the scale of our processing does not require one.

What we collect

  • Contact form. The fields you enter so we can reply: the reason you select, your email address, and — optionally — how many hosts you run, which features matter to you, and a free-text message. Only the email is required; the form does not ask for your name.
  • Email. If you write to hello@hostcd.dev or security@hostcd.dev, we receive your message and address.
  • Technical logs. Our hosting and network providers process standard request metadata (such as IP address and user-agent) to serve the site and protect it from abuse.
  • Usage analytics. We use a cookieless analytics service to count page visits and clicks on links, in aggregate, so we can see which pages are useful and improve them. It sets no cookie, builds no profile of you, does not track you across other websites, and does not receive data that identifies you.
  • Billing records. If you buy a licence, we process the details needed to issue and keep the invoice (payment itself is handled by the payment provider, not by this website).
  • Embedded demo video. The demo is embedded with click-to-load: nothing is requested from YouTube until you press Play. If you press Play, your browser connects to YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) to stream the video, and Google receives your IP address and player interactions under its own privacy policy. If you never press Play, no YouTube or Google request is made.

The only thing stored in your browser is a theme preference (light/dark) kept in localStorage. It stays on your device, is never sent to us, and is strictly necessary for the feature you asked for, so it does not require consent.

Who processes your data

We use a small number of providers that process data on our behalf or as part of delivering the site:

  • Formspree (US) — receives and delivers contact-form submissions to us. Acts as our processor. (privacy policy)
  • GitHub Pages — GitHub, Inc. (US) hosts and serves this website. (privacy statement)
  • Cloudflare, Inc. (US) — DNS and email routing for the domain, and privacy-first, cookieless web analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) that counts page visits and link clicks in aggregate, without cookies or cross-site tracking. (privacy policy)
  • YouTube / Google — Google Ireland Ltd. and Google LLC (US). The demo video is embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode and loads only after you click Play; at that point YouTube streams the video and receives your IP address and player data. We embed no YouTube cookies or scripts before you press Play. (privacy policy)

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with anyone else except where we must to comply with the law.

International transfers

The providers above are based in the United States, so processing your data involves a transfer outside the EEA. Each transfer is covered by an appropriate GDPR safeguard:

  • GitHub, Inc. — certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework; its Data Processing Agreement also incorporates the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. — certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, with the EU Standard Contractual Clauses included in its Data Processing Addendum as an additional basis.
  • Google LLC — certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework; its Standard Contractual Clauses provide an additional basis. This transfer only happens if you press Play on the embedded demo video.
  • Formspree — states that it relies on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses when acting as a data processor. We use its Free plan, under which submissions are retained in the Formspree dashboard for 30 days.

You can ask us for more detail about these safeguards at hello@hostcd.dev.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't lead to an agreement — the copy delivered to our mailbox, up to 12 months after our last exchange, then deleted (Formspree deletes its dashboard copy after 30 days).
  • Security reports — for the duration of the investigation and up to 24 months after it closes, unless longer retention is needed for a legal claim.
  • Technical logs — kept by our hosting and network providers for their own standard periods (typically days to a few weeks); we do not maintain a separate copy.
  • Invoices and tax records — for as long as Polish tax law requires (generally up to five years from the end of the relevant tax year).
  • Anything needed for a legal claim — until the relevant limitation period ends.

You can ask us to delete your data sooner at any time, subject to the retention we are legally required to keep.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased;
  • restrict how we process it;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest;
  • data portability, where it applies;
  • withdraw consent, where we rely on it.

To exercise any of these, email hello@hostcd.dev. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Polish supervisory authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO): uodo.gov.pl/en.
